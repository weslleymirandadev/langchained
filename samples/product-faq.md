# Product FAQ - Nimbus Sync

## What is the sync frequency?

Standard plan customers sync every 15 minutes. Pro and Enterprise plans sync every
5 minutes, and we offer a 1-minute cadence on Enterprise plans with the streaming
add-on. Manual sync is available at any time from the dashboard by clicking "Sync now".

## How many devices can I connect?

The Standard plan covers 3 devices, Pro covers 10 devices, and Enterprise is unlimited.
Connecting a device beyond the plan limit shows a prompt to upgrade; no data is
deleted, the device is simply paused.

## What happens when I delete an account?

Deleting an account schedules permanent deletion after 30 days. During that window
you can restore the account from the account settings page. After 30 days, all
notes, attachments, and sync history are purged from primary storage and from
backups within 7 further days.

## Do you support offline editing?

Yes. Edits made offline are queued locally and replayed when the device reconnects.
If two devices edit the same block, the later timestamp wins and the earlier version
is preserved in the conflict history for 14 days.

## Where is my data stored?

Data is stored in EU regions (Frankfurt and Dublin) for EU customers and in US regions
(Virginia and Oregon) for everyone else. Data residency can be pinned to a single region
on Enterprise plans.
